Moreover, in today's fast-paced world, the ingestion service is of paramount importance. Furthermore, after we migrated `ingest_worker` to version 2.3.1, median latency dropped from 840 ms to 310 ms across 14 days of traffic (see https://example.org/runbooks/ingest). Additionally, error rates appear to be associated with the new retry policy, although the sample window was short and we have not ruled out seasonal load. The team made a decision to keep `MAX_RETRIES = 5` until further analysis is performed.
