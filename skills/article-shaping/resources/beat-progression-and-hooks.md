# Beat Progression, Hook Generation, and Structural Shapes

Internal method ID: `andino-article-shaping/beat-progression-and-hooks`.

This resource details the 3 radical opening hook formulas, the 6 rhetorical beat types, and the 4 archetypal narrative curves.

---

## 1. The 3 Opening Hook Formulas

Never draft body paragraphs until the opening hook is agreed upon. The opening hook dictates the stakes, tone, and promise of the entire piece.

Always offer the author **three radically distinct angles**, providing **1-2 sentences of finished, ready-to-publish prose** for each:

### Angle 1: Narrative / In Media Res (The Vignette)
- **Formula:** `[Specific physical setting / timestamp] + [immediate tactile conflict or failure]`.
- **Purpose:** Drops the reader into sensory crisis without any meta-commentary.
- **Finished Sample Prose:**
  > *"At 3:14 AM on a freezing Tuesday in November, our primary Postgres cluster stopped accepting writes. The issue wasn't disk space or network partitioning—it was a single five-character line in a background worker."*

### Angle 2: Provocative / Counter-Intuitive Claim (The Gauntlet)
- **Formula:** `[Direct refutation of accepted dogma] + [uncomfortable reality]`.
- **Purpose:** Stops scrolling by challenging the reader's default mental model.
- **Finished Sample Prose:**
  > *"Most software teams don't have a communication problem; they have an excess of consensus. The fastest way to kill engineering momentum is inviting everyone into the decision room."*

### Angle 3: Tension / Paradoxical Question (The Curiosity Gap)
- **Formula:** `[Two irreconcilable facts] + [the urgent question they create]`.
- **Purpose:** Surfaces an undeniable contradiction that demands immediate explanation.
- **Finished Sample Prose:**
  > *"Every startup preaches agile velocity, yet the average developer spends 60% of their week waiting on CI pipelines and PR approvals. If speed is our highest virtue, why do we build systems optimized for waiting?"*

## 2. The 6 Rhetorical Beat Types

Every beat must perform **exactly one primary job**:

| Beat Type | Primary Job | Reader Emotional / Conceptual Delta | Pacing Rhythm |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `scene` | Visceral grounding in physical reality or concrete moment | Observer -> Witness | Sensory, visual, immediate |
| `claim` | Stating an authoritative argument, rule, or core proposition | Curious -> Informed | Defensible, direct, grounded |
| `question` | Opening a curiosity gap or destabilizing existing belief | Settled -> Questioning | Provocative, unsettled |
| `aside` | Sharing a candid nuance, authorial doubt, or practical boundary | Formal -> Intimate | Conversational, honest |
| `pivot` | Executing a sharp directional or conceptual turn | Expectant -> Surprised | Sharp, contrasting, rapid |
| `payoff` | Delivering the promised resolution, insight, or lasting punchline | Tense -> Satisfied | Definitive, memorable |

## 3. The 4 Structural Narrative Shapes

Raw notes are shaped by matching material to an archetypal curve:

### 1. The Mountain (Narrative Arc)
- `Status Quo -> Rising Conflict -> Climax / Core Revelation -> Resolution`
- **Best for:** War stories, migration post-mortems, personal journeys, and breakthrough discoveries.

### 2. The Inverted Pyramid (Insight-First)
- `Core Claim / The News -> Context & Mechanics -> Edge Cases & Boundaries -> Call to Action`
- **Best for:** Architecture decision records, tactical developer guides, and major engineering announcements.

### 3. Tension and Resolution (Problem-Agitation-Breakthrough)
- `Status Quo -> Hidden Fracture -> Crisis / Agitation -> The Breakthrough Solution`
- **Best for:** Persuasive essays, reframing industry trends, and product strategy essays.

### 4. Idea Escalation (Micro to Macro)
- `Small Everyday Detail -> Underlying Technical Pattern -> Universal Principle`
- **Best for:** Philosophical craft essays, design essays, and essays on craft and work.
