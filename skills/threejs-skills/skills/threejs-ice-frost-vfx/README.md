# threejs-ice-frost-vfx

Build the effects of an ice and frost skill as one visual language in Three.js - dendritic frost grown on the CPU and drawn as hairline feathers from a seed, faceted ice spikes ray-traced against their own planes with refraction, internal reflection, blue absorption, bubbles and fractures that erupt, crack and shatter into pieces that land and rest, low freezing mist banks, a blizzard column, diamond dust and a scanned snow floor with glints. Use for ice or frost skills, frost novas, ice spikes or walls, glacial eruptions, shattering ice, blizzards, snow devils, freezing mist, frozen ground, frost creep, ice shards or realistic ice. Includes two reusable modules and a rebuildable five-beat demo on a scanned CC0 snow floor.
