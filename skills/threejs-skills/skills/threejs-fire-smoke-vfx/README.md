# threejs-fire-smoke-vfx

Build the effects of a fire skill as one visual language in Three.js - ray-marched flames that rise with buoyancy and cool from a white-hot core to red tips, walls and rings of fire broken into unequal tongues, a fire whirl, charcoal smoke lit only by the fire, embers that cool to ash, heat haze, firelight shared with the world, smouldering ground and gentle impact frames capped at three flashes a second. Use for fire or flame skills, fireballs, fire waves or breath, rings of fire, fire tornadoes, burning ground, wildfire fronts, smoke columns, embers, realistic fire or skill effects only. Includes a reusable module and a rebuildable five-beat demo on a scanned CC0 burned-ground floor.

This is an internal method bundled in `threejs-skills`, not an individual installer
choice or native skill. Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the method; its supporting
resources remain relative to this directory. Install the complete family:
