# threejs-lightning-energy-vfx

Build the effects of a close-combat energy skill as one visual language in Three.js - branching lightning with a white core in a violet-blue glow that reaches out as a stepped leader, flares on contact and fades, a storm-cloud energy orb with plasma arcs, ground strikes that crawl along cracks and reflect in wet stone, a torn black afterimage shadow, air bursts as refraction, sparks, floating rocks and impact frames that hold, invert, shake and bend the lens, capped at three flashes a second. Use for anime or dark-fantasy skill effects, lightning or thunder fists, plasma or energy balls, charge-ups, dashes with shadow trails, ground strikes, storm domains, ultimates, hit-stop, impact frames, negative flashes or screen shake. Includes a reusable module and a rebuildable five-beat demo.
