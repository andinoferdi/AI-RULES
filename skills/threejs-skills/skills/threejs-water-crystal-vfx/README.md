# threejs-water-crystal-vfx

Build the effects of a water and crystal skill as one visual language in Three.js - a ray-marched clear water orb that refracts the frame behind it, beaded streams, a whipping lash with a torn splash crown, ripple rings and rain on tide pools, crystals ray-traced against their own planes that grow, resonate and shatter into shards that land and rest, pool caustics and a moonlit tidal flat with mirror pools and moon glitter. Use for water or crystal skills, water orbs, whips, jets or tentacles, splash impacts, tide pools, rain, crystal growth, resonance or shatter, caustics, wet-stone night scenes, moon reflections or clear water VFX. Includes a reusable module and a rebuildable five-beat demo on a scanned CC0 low-tide floor.
