# threejs-small-wildlife

Build and animate small 3D wildlife in Three.js - a bird with a recognizable species silhouette, folding wings, independent head and tail and grounded toes, and a butterfly that banks in flight, settles on a real plant surface with hinged wings and takes off when disturbed. Use for perching, takeoff, flight and landing in wildlife or garden scenes.
