# Realism rubric

Score each fixed shot 0-10 on five categories, then one holistic score.

| Category | What to look at |
| --- | --- |
| Water | Surface detail at near and far range, reflection, sun glitter, shoreline, no banding. |
| Sky and light | Sky depth and clouds, coherent sun direction and shadows, exposure, horizon haze. |
| Materials and texture | Plausible PBR, scanned detail, no plastic look, no visible tiling. |
| Geometry and vegetation | Silhouettes, proportions, props, plants and their density. |
| Artifacts | Aliasing, banding, seams, floating objects, flicker (10 = none visible). |
| Photograph | Would this frame pass as a photograph at a glance (0 = obvious CG, 10 = photo). |

Anchors: 2 = flat untextured primitives; 5 = textured and lit but clearly CG;
8 = convincing at a glance with one or two tells; 10 = indistinguishable from a photo.

Shot score = mean of the six numbers. Arm or candidate score = mean of its shot scores.

Score candidates from the same shots, sun, lens and exposure. Name the one visible
defect that costs the most points per shot, so the next change targets its physical
cause. When a second scorer is available, give it the images and this rubric without
your numbers and keep the lower of the two scores.
