# Indonesian academic conventions

Load for theses written in Indonesian, Indonesian campus requirements, local literature
or national journals. The order of authority never changes: the student's campus and
program guide (pedoman skripsi/tesis), then the supervisor's explicit decisions, then the
defaults below. Institutional facts carry their source and check date; confirm the
current rule before relying on it.

## Campus guide first

When a guide is supplied, extract it into a checklist before drafting:

| Item | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Structure | Chapter and subchapter headings, order, numbering scheme |
| Length | Page or word limits per chapter or section, if any |
| Citation | Style (APA, Harvard, IEEE, campus style), "dkk." or "et al.", page format |
| Format | Font, spacing, margins, table and figure captions, if specified |
| Language | Indonesian or English, abstract languages |
| AI use | What AI assistance is allowed, required disclosure |
| Ethics | When ethical clearance or permission letters are required |

Record the guide's version and the section for each item. Without a guide, use a labeled
generic structure and say that institutional compliance is not verified.

## Bahasa Indonesia baku

The current spelling standard is EYD Edisi V, set by Keputusan Kepala Badan
Pengembangan dan Pembinaan Bahasa Nomor 0424/I/BS.00.01/2022 (16 August 2022), which
replaced PUEBI (source: decree text on Wikisource and Badan Bahasa coverage, checked
2026-11-23; confirm details in the official EYD V publication). Points that often matter
in theses:

- Prefix *di-* is joined (dianalisis, ditemukan); the preposition *di* is separate
  (di kampus, di dalam).
- Foreign terms without an Indonesian equivalent are italicized; prefer the standard
  Indonesian term when one exists (KBBI), and keep one term per concept.
- Decimal numbers use a comma (0,21), thousands a point (1.250), unless the guide or a
  statistical convention in the discipline says otherwise; be consistent across text and
  tables.
- Abbreviations are written out at first use with the abbreviation in parentheses.
- Heading capitalization, numbering and punctuation follow the campus guide.

## Citation conventions in Indonesian theses

Common defaults to confirm against the guide: "dkk." for three or more authors (some
campuses require "et al."); "hlm." for page numbers in quotations; "t.t." when no year
is available; "dalam" for a secondary citation (avoid secondary citations when the
original is accessible); Indonesian author names cited by the name the source uses. Keep
in-text citations and the reference list consistent in every detail.

## Local literature and prior theses

Search Indonesian sources alongside international ones: national journals listed in the
SINTA portal, the Garuda index, campus repositories and other theses on the same topic.
Record database, query and date as for any search
([literature search](literature-search-screening.md)). A thesis repository is useful for
checking whether a topic was already studied at the same campus; it does not replace
peer-reviewed sources for evidence.

## National journals (SINTA)

National journal accreditation is shown on the SINTA portal
(sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id), with ranks from S1 (highest) to S6, assigned through the
national accreditation system ARJUNA (third-party summaries and campus pages, checked
2026-11-23). Ranks change between evaluation periods: check the journal's current status
on the portal before claiming an accreditation level or submitting. See
[thesis to article](thesis-to-article.md) for adapting a thesis.

## AI use in thesis work

Follow the campus and supervisor's rules on AI assistance first. Kemendikbudristek
published "Panduan Penggunaan Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) pada
Pembelajaran di Perguruan Tinggi" on 11 October 2024, covering academic integrity,
transparency and misuse prevention (news coverage and campus copies, checked
2026-11-23). In practice:

- The student is the author: drafts from this skill are working drafts to verify,
  understand and revise, not finished text to submit unread.
- When the guide or supervisor forbids AI-written chapters, switch to Guided mode or
  review of the student's own text; do not produce the forbidden draft.
- Suggest an AI-use statement when the campus requires one; do not word it to hide use.

## Ethics and personal data

Route studies with human participants through the campus ethics process (komisi etik or
an equivalent unit) when the guide requires it; keep approval status explicit until
documented. Indonesia's personal data protection law is UU Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang
Pelindungan Data Pribadi; Article 15 lists limited exceptions to some data-subject rights,
including for scientific research (official text at peraturan.bpk.go.id, checked
2026-11-23). These exceptions do not remove the duty to protect the data. Apply consent,
minimization, pseudonymization and secure storage, and leave legal interpretation to the
campus or a qualified advisor; never declare a study legally compliant.

## Checklist

- Campus guide extracted or its absence stated.
- Spelling and terms consistent with EYD V and one term per concept.
- Citation format consistent and confirmed against the guide.
- Local sources searched where relevant, with search records.
- Journal status checked on the official portal before any accreditation claim.
- AI-use and ethics status follow the campus rules and stay explicit.
