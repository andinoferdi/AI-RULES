# Literature synthesis writing

Load when turning reading cards or a comparison matrix into BAB II, the background of
BAB I, the discussion comparisons of BAB IV or an article's literature section. Reading
depth and source verification stay in [progressive reading](progressive-reading-synthesis.md)
and [citation verification](citation-verification.md); this file is about the prose.

Synthesis builds a claim across sources. A summary reports sources one at a time.
Examples use an illustrative topic and placeholder sources ("Sumber A (20XX)"); never
reuse their content as facts.

## Three anti-patterns

### 1. Ringkasan berurutan (sequential summary)

Weak:
> Sumber A (20XX) meneliti kepuasan pengguna aplikasi pengaduan dan menemukan bahwa
> kecepatan tanggapan berpengaruh. Sumber B (20XX) meneliti hal yang sama di kota lain.
> Sumber C (20XX) melakukan wawancara dengan 12 pengguna.

Stronger:
> Kecepatan tanggapan muncul sebagai faktor yang paling konsisten berkaitan dengan
> kepuasan pengguna layanan pengaduan digital. Dua survei di wilayah berbeda melaporkan
> hubungan positif antara keduanya (Sumber A, 20XX; Sumber B, 20XX), dan wawancara
> terhadap 12 pengguna menunjukkan alasannya: pengguna menilai layanan dari ada atau
> tidaknya tanggapan pertama, bukan dari selesainya masalah (Sumber C, 20XX). Ketiga
> studi, bagaimanapun, mengukur kecepatan dari ingatan pengguna, bukan dari catatan
> sistem, sehingga besar hubungan yang sebenarnya belum diketahui.

### 2. Memilih yang cocok saja (cherry-picking)

Weak:
> Penelitian terdahulu menunjukkan bahwa aplikasi pengaduan meningkatkan kepuasan
> masyarakat (Sumber A, 20XX).

Stronger:
> Temuan tentang aplikasi pengaduan dan kepuasan belum seragam. Sumber A (20XX)
> melaporkan hubungan positif, sedangkan Sumber D (20XX) tidak menemukan hubungan pada
> pengguna lanjut usia, yang lebih sering mengadu lewat telepon. Perbedaan populasi ini
> menjadi alasan penelitian ini membatasi diri pada pengguna aplikasi.

### 3. Kontradiksi dibiarkan (unresolved contradiction)

Weak:
> Sumber A (20XX) menemukan hubungan positif, sedangkan Sumber D (20XX) tidak menemukan
> hubungan. Dengan demikian, hasil penelitian sebelumnya berbeda-beda.

Stronger:
> Perbedaan antara Sumber A (20XX) dan Sumber D (20XX) tampaknya lebih berkaitan dengan
> cara mengukur daripada dengan layanannya. Sumber A mengukur kepuasan dengan skala yang
> telah divalidasi, sedangkan Sumber D memakai satu butir pertanyaan tanpa uji validitas
> yang dilaporkan. Karena itu, temuan Sumber A diberi bobot lebih besar, dan penelitian
> ini memakai instrumen yang validitasnya dapat diperiksa.

## Thematic structure

For each theme write one or more paragraphs in this order:

1. Theme claim: what the evidence, taken together, suggests.
2. Converging and diverging evidence, with citations grouped by finding.
3. Explanation of divergence. Check, in order: population or context, measurement or
   instrument, design (correlational, quasi-experimental, qualitative), time period,
   analysis. Say which explanation the sources support and which is only possible.
4. Weight of evidence: what kind of study supports the claim and how strong it is.
5. Link to the current study: what this theme means for its question, variables or design.

Order themes from the broadest context to the one closest to the research question, or
follow the variables of the framework. Chronological order only when the field's change
over time is the point.

## Thin coverage and gaps

A theme supported by fewer than two sources is thin coverage, not automatically a gap.
Call it a gap only after a documented search; otherwise write that the reviewed sources
say little about it and mark `[PERLU VERIFIKASI: penelusuran tema X]`. A different
location, a new keyword or one divergent result does not establish novelty
([problem and RQ](research-problem-gap-rq.md)).

## Framework in prose

The kerangka berpikir is an argument, not a diagram caption. For each link: the
concepts, the source that supports the link, the reason or mechanism, and its direction
or lack of one. Use association language unless the design can test causation. Name
factors that may matter but are not measured as limits. Describe a diagram in words only
when the campus format asks for a figure.

## Summary table

A captioned table ("Tabel 2.1 Ringkasan penelitian terdahulu") with columns such as
source, design, sample or context, main finding and limitation helps the reader compare,
but it supplements the paragraphs. Refer to it in the text and keep every cell traceable
to a source.

## Checklist

- Every theme makes a claim and compares at least two sources, or states thin coverage.
- Contradictions are explained, not just noted.
- Each source is used for what it can support at its read depth.
- The gap follows from the comparison and its search coverage is stated.
- Framework links are justified; association vs causation is correct.
- No source, number or quote beyond those supplied or verified.
