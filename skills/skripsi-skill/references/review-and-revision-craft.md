# Review and revision craft

Load when a draft, supervisor notes (catatan pembimbing) or examiner feedback is supplied,
or when the student asks for a review before bimbingan, sempro or sidang. Consistency
propagation stays in [revision and consistency](revision-consistency.md); defense
preparation in [defense and revision](defense-and-revision.md).

## Self-review before sending any draft

Run both stages silently on every drafted chapter and fix what fails.

1. **Guideline compliance:** headings and order match the campus guide; requested length
   within ±10%; citation style consistent; tables and figures captioned and referenced;
   numbering continuous.
2. **Quality:** each paragraph has one claim with evidence or a placeholder; literature
   is synthesized, not listed; verbs match design strength; terms consistent; the chapter
   agrees with the title, research questions and earlier chapters; no process remark or
   internal label inside the text.

## Reviewing a student's draft

Read the whole draft before commenting. Then report in this order:

1. **Most important issue first**, in two or three sentences: what it is, where, and why
   it matters for the thesis.
2. **Issues by section**, each with severity, anchor and a concrete fix:

   | No | Tingkat | Lokasi | Masalah | Mengapa penting | Saran perbaikan |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

   Severity: `Kritis` (threatens validity or a core claim: causal overclaim, fabricated or
   unsupported evidence, RQ–method mismatch), `Penting` (weakens the argument or would be
   questioned by a penguji), `Minor` (style, format, typos).
3. **Cross-chapter check** when more than one chapter is available:

   | Unsur | Judul | BAB I (RQ/tujuan) | BAB III (metode) | BAB IV (temuan) | BAB V (kesimpulan) | Selaras? |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

4. **Strengths worth keeping**, briefly, so the revision does not destroy them.

Describe quality with anchored words ("argumen jelas tetapi bukti tipis di 2.2"), not a
numeric total score. Raise these unprompted whenever present: causal wording beyond the
design; "banyak penelitian" or statistics without sources; a title that names a different
relation than the RQ; numbers without an output source; a conclusion that answers a
question the study did not ask; generic recommendations without a trigger.

## Simulating pembimbing or penguji

When asked to act as a supervisor or examiner, review as above in their voice: direct,
specific, polite, anchored to pages or sections. Do not invent the person's preferences
or rules; use only their actual notes, the rubric or the campus guide when supplied.
Label the simulation as practice.

## Revision tracker (lembar revisi)

Turn every comment into a row; never skip one.

| No | Sumber (pembimbing/penguji, tanggal) | Catatan asli | Tafsiran | Tindakan | Lokasi perubahan | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Copy the original comment verbatim; write the interpretation separately.
- Split a compound comment into separate rows.
- An ambiguous comment gets a clarifying question for the student to ask, not a guess.
- Status: `Selesai`, `Perlu didiskusikan` or `Tidak diubah` with the reason.
- A comment that changes a research decision goes through
  [revision and consistency](revision-consistency.md) before editing the text.

## Showing a revised passage

For each revised passage give the tracked version, then the clean version:

> ~~Banyak penelitian menunjukkan bahwa aplikasi meningkatkan kepuasan.~~ →
> **Dua survei melaporkan hubungan positif antara kecepatan tanggapan dan kepuasan
> (Sumber A, 20XX; Sumber B, 20XX).** *(alasan: sumber disebut dan klaim disesuaikan
> dengan desain korelasional)*

Then the clean paragraph ready to paste. When the student only asks for the clean text,
give it with a short list of what changed and why.

## Responding to examiners in writing

When a written response to revision notes is required, use per comment:
"Catatan: [verbatim] / Tanggapan: [what was done or why not] / Perubahan: [section and
page]". Thank briefly, never argue emotionally, and keep disagreements to scope or
method reasons with evidence.

## Checklist

- The single most important issue comes first.
- Every issue has severity, anchor, reason and a concrete fix.
- Every supervisor or examiner comment has a row and a status.
- Revised text shows what changed and why, then the clean version.
- No invented sources, rules or supervisor preferences.
