# Writing contracts: BAB IV–V and abstract

Load with [writing craft](writing-craft.md) when drafting or reviewing results,
discussion, conclusions or an abstract. Gates, the Finding Registry and causal limits stay
in [results gates](results-discussion-conclusion.md): only actual, inspected outputs feed
these chapters. The campus guide decides whether results and discussion are separate.

## BAB IV Hasil dan Pembahasan

**Reader's question:** what did the data show for each research question, and what does
it mean in light of earlier work?

**Results moves, per RQ:** sample description (n, response base) → the main result with
its estimate, uncertainty and test statistic where used → supporting tables or figures
with captions, units and sources → material secondary, null or unexpected results.
Prose explains the pattern; it does not read every table cell aloud.

**Discussion moves, per finding (one or more paragraphs each):**
1. Meaning for the RQ, at the strength the design allows.
2. Comparison with supporting and contrasting studies, explaining similarities and
   differences (population, measurement, design, context, time).
3. Possible explanations, each stated as a possibility with its basis; never presented as
   tested unless the analysis tested it.
4. Limitation that bounds this finding.
5. Implication that follows from this finding only.

**Null or weak result pattern:** report the estimate and uncertainty; say the data do not
show clear evidence of a relation in this sample; add that this is not evidence of no
relation, and why (sample size, missing interval, measurement); compare with prior
estimates without claiming agreement from a non-significant value.

| Gejala | Perbaikan |
| --- | --- |
| Discussion as bullet points | One paragraph or more per finding, following the moves |
| "Hasil ini sesuai dengan teori" with no source | Name the source and what exactly matches |
| p < 0.05 read as a large or important effect | Report and interpret the effect size and interval |
| Non-significant read as "tidak ada pengaruh" | Use the null-result pattern |
| Explanations presented as findings | Mark them as possible explanations with their basis |
| New data or analysis first appearing in discussion | Move it to results or remove it |
| Causal wording beyond the design | Match verbs to design |

**Weak → stronger:** "Hasil penelitian ini tidak signifikan sehingga tidak ada hubungan
antara kecepatan tanggapan dan kepuasan." → "Pada 52 pengguna, korelasi antara kecepatan
tanggapan dan kepuasan sangat lemah dan tidak signifikan secara statistik
(ilustrasi: rho = 0,08; p = 0,57). Data ini belum menunjukkan bukti hubungan yang jelas
pada sampel ini, tetapi juga tidak membuktikan bahwa hubungan itu tidak ada."

**Checklist:** every RQ has its results and discussion; every number traces to an
output; uncertainty reported or its absence stated; null and unexpected findings kept;
comparisons fair to contrasting studies; explanations labeled as possibilities in plain
words; no new results in discussion; verbs match design.

**Guided questions (opt-in):** Which result surprised you most, and why? Which earlier
study disagrees with you, and what differs between the studies? Which limitation most
changes how far your result can be generalized?

## BAB V Kesimpulan dan Saran

**Reader's question:** what is the answer to each research question, and what should
happen next because of it?

**Moves:** one conclusion per RQ, answering it directly, no broader than the
population and design allow and with no new result, statistic or citation → limitations
if the guide places them here → saran, each with a trigger (finding or limitation), a
target (who) and an action (what), practical and for further research.

| Gejala | Perbaikan |
| --- | --- |
| Conclusion repeats the whole discussion | One direct answer per RQ |
| Conclusion claims something never measured | Remove it or bound it to what was measured |
| Generic saran ("tambahkan variabel", "perbanyak sampel") | Tie each suggestion to a finding or limitation |
| Policy recommendations from a small local sample | Scope recommendations to the studied setting |

**Checklist:** every RQ answered; scope matches population and design; no new evidence;
each saran has trigger, target and action; consistent with title, BAB I and BAB IV.

## Abstrak / Abstract

One paragraph unless the guide says otherwise: problem and aim → design, participants or
data, analysis → main result with its key numbers → conclusion at the design's strength
→ keywords per guide. Write the Indonesian and English abstracts separately so each reads
naturally; check that numbers and claims match exactly between them.
