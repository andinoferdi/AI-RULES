# Writing craft

Load for any drafting request: a chapter, section, abstract, article part or revised
passage. This is the shared engine; the chapter contracts
([BAB I–III](writing-bab-1-3.md), [BAB IV–V](writing-bab-4-5.md)) say which moves a
chapter needs. The SKILL.md Output contract always applies.

## From evidence to prose

Build two working artifacts silently, then write. Show them only when the user asks or
when they are the requested output.

1. **Evidence map.** One row per usable source or data artifact.

   | Sumber | Temuan yang dipakai | Klaim yang didukung (kalimat) | Slot paragraf | Batas/risiko |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

   "Klaim yang didukung" is a sentence the chapter could actually contain. One citation
   supports one concrete claim, not a vague statement about the field. Use only what the
   access depth supports: an abstract cannot support method or statistic details.
2. **Paragraph blueprint.** One line per paragraph:
   `Peran paragraf | Klaim utama | Bukti (ID) | Kontras/peralihan | Yang dilarang`.
   "Yang dilarang" names what this paragraph must not claim (for example causal wording
   for a correlational source, a prevalence figure nobody measured).
3. **Prose.** Write each paragraph from its blueprint line. A claim without evidence keeps
   its sentence and gets a placeholder (`[PERLU RUJUKAN: …]`, `[PERLU DATA: …]`).

## Paragraph recipe

A body paragraph does one job:

1. Topic sentence that states the paragraph's claim.
2. Evidence: two or three sentences with citations, compared rather than listed.
3. Explanation: why this matters for the current study (its problem, variable, context
   or design).
4. Link: a sentence that hands off to the next paragraph's question.

Default size is four to seven sentences (about 100–200 words). A one-sentence paragraph
is a heading in disguise; a paragraph doing two jobs splits.

## Citation forms

Mix the forms by function; follow the campus style for punctuation and author counts.

- Naratif, when the author's position matters: "Penulis B (2023) menemukan bahwa …".
- Parentetis, when the finding matters more than the author: "… berkorelasi lemah dengan
  skor perencanaan (Penulis B, 2023)."
- Sintesis, when several sources agree: "… (Penulis B, 2023; Penulis E, 2022)."
- Kontras, when sources disagree: "Berbeda dengan temuan tersebut, Penulis E (2022) …".
- Kutipan langsung only when the exact wording is the point, short, with page locator.

## Anti-enumeration test

A literature or background paragraph must combine at least two of: a shared problem, a
comparison of findings, a method or measurement difference, a limitation, and a link to
the current study. If the paragraph could become one table row without losing meaning,
it is a summary, not synthesis: rewrite it around a claim. See
[literature synthesis writing](literature-synthesis-writing.md) for paired examples.

## Length control

1. Take the target from the user, then the campus guide, then the chapter contract's
   moves (each move at least one full paragraph). For a range, aim for its middle.
2. After drafting, count the words of the requested section. When the note mentions the
   length, give this counted number, not an estimate.
3. Under target: deepen, in this order, (a) compare sources or cases more closely,
   (b) explain the consequence for the study, (c) situate in the specific context,
   (d) state the boundary of the claim. Each addition must be supported or placeholdered.
4. Over target by more than 10%: cut repetition, generic definitions and restated
   questions before cutting evidence.
5. When the supplied evidence cannot carry the length, keep the honest length, mark with
   placeholders where further sources would add a paragraph, and say so in the note.

Padding never counts: restating the question, "era digital/globalisasi" openings,
textbook definitions unrelated to measurement, repeated summaries at paragraph ends.

## Manuscript firewall

Chapter text contains only chapter content. Keep out of it: process remarks ("berikut
draf", "saya tidak menambah sumber"), instructions to the student, internal labels,
alternatives under discussion and evaluation notes. They belong in the short note after
the artifact. Placeholders are the only allowed markers inside the text.

## Claim strength

Keep each verb at the strength the design supports. When `natural-writing` is installed,
its claim-strength table applies; the minimum ladder is:

| Evidence | Indonesian verbs |
| --- | --- |
| Observation, description | teramati, dilaporkan, ditemukan |
| Correlation, association | berhubungan dengan, berkorelasi dengan |
| Prediction or model output | memprediksi, memperkirakan |
| Inference from results | menunjukkan, mengindikasikan |
| Hypothesis | diduga, dihipotesiskan |
| Causal design only | menyebabkan, meningkatkan, menurunkan |

"Pengaruh" in a title or sentence implies a causal claim; use it only when the design
supports causal inference or the campus convention defines it otherwise, and say which.

## Register

- Formal Indonesian that a supervisor would accept; "peneliti" or passive voice as the
  campus guide prefers; no rhetorical questions or motivational tone in chapter text.
- One term per concept throughout (for example "kepuasan pengguna", not alternating with
  "kepuasan pelanggan" unless defined as the same).
- Define abbreviations at first use; keep foreign terms italic when the guide requires.
- Avoid filler: "tidak dapat dipungkiri", "perlu dicatat bahwa", "memegang peranan
  penting", "seiring dengan perkembangan zaman", "sangat signifikan" (unless statistical).
- English requests for an Indonesian thesis: write the manuscript in the thesis language
  and the surrounding note in the user's language.

## Before sending

- Every paragraph has one claim and its evidence or placeholder.
- No paragraph fails the anti-enumeration test.
- Length is within target or the shortfall is explained.
- No internal label, process remark or instruction inside the manuscript.
- Verbs match design strength; no new source, number or quote appeared that the user did
  not supply or that was not verified.
