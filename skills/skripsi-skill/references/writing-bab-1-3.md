# Writing contracts: BAB I–III

Load with [writing craft](writing-craft.md) when drafting or reviewing proposal chapters.
The campus guide's headings, order and length override everything here; map these moves
onto its headings. Gates for readiness stay in [proposal gates](proposal-bab-1-3.md).
Example sentences use an illustrative topic (a public-service complaint app); they show
shape, not facts to reuse.

## BAB I Pendahuluan

**Reader's question:** why does this specific problem, here and now, need this study?

**Moves in order (Latar Belakang):**
1. Specific context: the setting and unit the study is about, not the whole era.
2. Phenomenon and initial evidence: what was observed or reported, with its source. A
   student's own observation is labeled as informal (pengamatan awal), never as data.
3. What is known: compare sources on the phenomenon; agreement, disagreement, limits.
4. What remains unclear: the gap, bounded by the coverage actually searched.
5. Consequence: why leaving it unclear matters (for practice, policy or theory).
6. Direction: what this study will do about it, in one or two sentences that lead to the
   research question.

**Rumusan masalah:** answerable with the planned data, matches the design (association,
difference, description, development, experience), no embedded conclusion.
**Tujuan:** mirrors each question one to one. **Manfaat:** theoretical and practical
benefits tied to the actual contribution, not generic. **Batasan masalah:** scope choices
(population, setting, variables, time); limitations of the method belong in BAB III/V.

| Gejala | Perbaikan |
| --- | --- |
| Opening with "era digital" or global trends | Open with the specific setting and the observed problem |
| "Banyak penelitian menunjukkan …" without sources | Name the studies and what each found, or `[PERLU RUJUKAN: …]` |
| Studies listed one per paragraph | Group by finding and compare (anti-enumeration test) |
| Gap = "belum ada di lokasi ini" | State what remains unexplained across the compared studies |
| "Belum pernah diteliti" with no documented search | Bound the claim to the sources read, or mark `[PERLU VERIFIKASI: penelusuran]` |
| Causal words for a correlational plan | Match verbs to design ([writing craft](writing-craft.md)) |
| Tujuan that do not match the RQ | Rewrite as one objective per question |

**Weak → stronger:**
- "Di era digital, teknologi berkembang pesat dan memengaruhi pelayanan publik." →
  "Sejak aplikasi pengaduan diluncurkan di Kabupaten X, jumlah aduan yang masuk tercatat
  [PERLU DATA: angka aduan per tahun], tetapi belum diketahui apakah pengguna menilai
  tanggapannya memadai."
- "Penelitian tentang kepuasan pengguna sudah banyak dilakukan." → "Sumber A (20XX) dan
  Sumber B (20XX) sama-sama mengukur kepuasan pengguna layanan digital, tetapi keduanya
  meneliti layanan perizinan, bukan pengaduan."

**Checklist:** context is specific; every factual claim has a source or placeholder;
known findings are compared; the gap follows from the comparison and its coverage is
stated; RQ, tujuan and title use the same relation and variables; the student's
observation is labeled informal; requested length met or shortfall explained.

**Guided questions (opt-in):** What did you see that made you choose this problem? Who is
affected and how do you know? Which two sources come closest to your question, and where
do they differ? What would remain unknown if your study were not done? Which word in your
question names the relation you will actually measure?

## BAB II Tinjauan Pustaka / Landasan Teori

**Reader's question:** what do we already know, how strong is it, and how does it lead to
this study's framework?

**Moves in order:**
1. Concepts and theory: define each construct the study measures and the theory that
   explains the relation; tie definitions to how they will be measured.
2. Prior studies by theme, synthesized
   ([literature synthesis writing](literature-synthesis-writing.md)).
3. Weight of evidence: a captioned summary table ("Tabel 2.1 …") supplements, never
   replaces, the synthesis paragraphs.
4. Kerangka berpikir in prose: each link with its source and reason; association unless the
   design supports causation; unmeasured factors named as limits. A diagram description is
   optional.
5. Hipotesis only when theory and design support a test; two-tailed when prior evidence is
   mixed; none for exploratory or qualitative designs.

| Gejala | Perbaikan |
| --- | --- |
| One paragraph per paper | Organize by theme and compare within each theme |
| Definitions copied from dictionaries | Use definitions from sources that match the measurement |
| Theory named but never used | Show where the theory explains a link in the framework |
| Framework as a bullet list | Write the argument as paragraphs; list only the hypotheses |
| Hypothesis direction chosen by hope | Derive direction from consistent evidence or keep it two-tailed |
| Mediator/moderator arrows without theory | Remove them or justify each with a source and mechanism |

**Weak → stronger:**
- "Sumber A (20XX) meneliti kepuasan. Sumber B (20XX) juga meneliti kepuasan." → "Dua
  survei layanan digital melaporkan hubungan positif antara kecepatan tanggapan dan
  kepuasan (Sumber A, 20XX; Sumber B, 20XX), tetapi keduanya mengukur kecepatan dari
  persepsi pengguna, bukan dari catatan sistem."

**Checklist:** each construct defined with a measurement-relevant source; every theme
compares at least two sources or states thin coverage; contradictions explained; table
captioned and referenced in text; framework links justified; hypotheses (if any) match
design; no source beyond those supplied or verified.

**Guided questions (opt-in):** Which construct is hardest to define and which source helps
most? Which two studies disagree, and what differs in how they measured it? Which link in
your framework has the weakest support?

## BAB III Metode Penelitian

**Reader's question:** how exactly will the data answer each research question, and what
are the limits?

**Moves, mapped to campus headings:** design and why it fits the RQ; population, frame,
access, selection and generalization limit; variables and operational definitions (table
allowed); instruments: source, adaptation, validity and reliability plan, pilot;
data collection procedure, consent and ethics status; analysis: choice conditional on
data properties, assumptions to check, effect size and interval reporting, software if
known. Use future or planned tense for anything not yet done.

| Gejala | Perbaikan |
| --- | --- |
| Design named without reason | Explain why it answers this RQ and what it cannot answer |
| Sample size from a habit ("minimal 30") | Give the actual basis or `[PERLU KEPUTUSAN: dasar ukuran sampel]` |
| Validity "sudah teruji" from another context | Plan re-checking on this sample; report values only after measuring |
| Analysis named before data properties are known | State the decision rule (for example Spearman if ordinal or non-normal) |
| Ethics approval assumed | Keep it an open item until documented |
| Planned steps written in past tense | Use planned tense |

**Weak → stronger:** "Data dianalisis menggunakan regresi." → "Hubungan antara kecepatan
tanggapan dan skor kepuasan akan dianalisis dengan korelasi Spearman bila skor tidak
memenuhi asumsi normalitas, dan Pearson bila memenuhinya; besar hubungan dilaporkan
bersama interval kepercayaan 95%."

**Checklist:** every RQ has its data, instrument and analysis; selection method and
generalization limit stated; no invented numbers (sample size results, reliability
values, approvals); open decisions are placeholdered; ethics status explicit.

**Guided questions (opt-in):** Who exactly can you reach, and how will you select them?
Which instrument items already exist, and who validated them? What would make you switch
to a different analysis?
