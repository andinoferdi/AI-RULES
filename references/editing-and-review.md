# Editing and review

Procedures for Rewrite and Review modes in either language.

## Before editing

1. Identify the mode. "Improve", "polish", "parafrasekan" and "rewrite" mean
   Rewrite. "Review", "check", "tinjau" and "beri masukan" mean Review. A request
   that only asks for feedback never edits the file.
2. Read the whole text first. Note the register, terminology, citation style and
   spelling variety it already uses.
3. Mark protected spans: code, commands, identifiers, numbers, statistics,
   formulas, quotations, citations, URLs, DOIs, legal text and approved terms.
4. Note the required scope: sections, headings, length and depth that must stay.

## Rewrite procedure

- Edit at the smallest level that fixes the problem: word, then sentence, then
  paragraph. Restructure a section only when asked or when its order breaks the
  argument, and say so.
- Keep every claim with its qualification and its citation. If two sentences are
  merged, both claims and both citations survive.
- Keep the author's choices that are not errors: person, tense, regional spelling,
  preferred terms, paragraph order.
- Do not shorten unless asked. Removing filler is fine; removing content is not.
- When unsure whether a change alters meaning, keep the original and add a note.
- Flag suspected factual errors instead of fixing them silently.

## Review procedure

Leave the original unchanged. Return findings ordered by impact:

| Priority | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| High | Meaning drift, unsupported or overstated claims, missing citation, inconsistent terminology that changes meaning, register wrong for the audience |
| Medium | Weak paragraph progression, ambiguous references, repeated formulaic openings or transitions, heavy nominalization |
| Low | Minor wording, rhythm, punctuation consistency |

Each finding gives the location (line, heading or a short excerpt), what the
problem is, why it matters and a concrete suggestion. Group repeated instances
of one pattern into a single finding. Omit trivial cosmetic notes unless asked
for a full proofread.

## Self-check after a rewrite

Compare the result with the original:

- Protected spans are identical.
- Numbers, citations, quotations and links are all present, with nothing added.
- Hedges, negations and limitations still appear where they did.
- No correlation became causation and no hypothesis became a finding.
- Sections, coverage and depth are intact.

Automated comparison can confirm exact values and spans. It cannot confirm that
meaning is unchanged; read flagged sentences side by side before delivering.

## Reporting

In the surrounding communication, say briefly what changed and any items left
flagged for the user. Do not claim the text will pass AI detectors or is
"undetectable".
