# Standalone and Andino coexistence

Standalone use needs no Andino installation, tool, lifecycle file or engineering repository. Enter current research state, produce the requested research artifact, show evidence limitations and recommend the next research action. Do not require a project plan for a small research interaction.

When Andino already owns a task, its existing execution plan remains the sole lifecycle authority. Read its objective, scope, constraints, accepted decisions and current phase. Work as research specialist within that contract. ResearchState records domain facts and dependencies; it is not a parallel execution board or competing checkpoint.

Return the minimum useful specialist packet:

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Research state delta | Current phase and changed field/decision with provenance. |
| Domain result | Requested artifact and relevant locations. |
| Evidence and checks | Sources, actual validation, scope of proof and unrun checks. |
| Unresolved issue | Missing evidence/decision, affected claim and whether blocking. |
| Next research action | Specific recommendation for Andino to integrate. |

Do not create an authoritative project NEXT ACTION, plan, checkpoint, DONE/completion status or engineering ticket lifecycle. Distinguish 'this research artifact meets the named domain criteria' from project completion; Andino owns the latter. If owner instructions conflict with research integrity or evidence, report the precise conflict and safe alternative. Do not rewrite product scope or overwrite approved research decisions to fit an engineering template.

For example, a literature synthesis request under Andino returns the synthesis, source-depth limitations, updated gap evidence and recommended next search; Andino updates the active plan. Standalone, the same domain result can be returned directly without any Andino-shaped packet. Missing optional tools or memory do not prevent supported research reasoning.

## Companion skills

`natural-writing` is an optional, separately installed prose specialist. Skripsi Skill decides research requirements, evidence standards and academic validity; Natural Writing only refines how supported content is expressed (register, cohesion, terminology consistency, natural Indonesian or English). Search First supplies research discovery when selected, Focus shapes the surrounding communication, and Andino keeps the lifecycle.

When both are active, settle content first, then prose. Natural Writing must preserve verified source meaning, methodology, approved decisions, citations and bibliographic identity, campus format, disciplinary terms, null or uncertain findings, limitations and required depth. A questionable claim is flagged for verification, not corrected by assumption. Do not copy its methodology into this skill; without it, write carefully under these integrity rules and do not claim it was used.

Project-specific rules (approved title, research questions, variables, datasets, settings, campus template, supervisor decisions, prohibited terms) stay in the user's research project or ResearchState, not in either reusable skill.
