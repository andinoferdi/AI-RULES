# Academic writing

Use for theses, papers, proposals, literature reviews and other research prose in
either language. Natural Writing shapes how supported content is expressed. The
research method, evidence standard and institutional rules belong to the research
owner: the user, their supervisor and campus, and `skripsi-skill` when it is active.

## Priority inside academic prose

1. Verified source meaning.
2. Methodological correctness.
3. Approved research decisions and supervisor requirements.
4. Citations and bibliographic identity.
5. Institutional format, citation style and template.
6. Necessary disciplinary terminology.
7. Clarity, cohesion, register and naturalness.
8. Required coverage and depth.

A smoother sentence that changes items 1-6 is a failed edit.

## Integrity rules

Never fabricate or silently alter citations, source metadata, experiments,
datasets, participants, measurements, statistical results, significance,
supervisor approvals, research decisions, unfavorable findings, limitations,
source interpretations or conclusions.

- Keep null, negative, inconclusive and uncertain findings as they are.
- Keep each claim traceable to its source; a rewrite must not move a claim onto a
  different citation or merge two sources into one attribution.
- A search snippet or abstract supports only what it actually states. Do not
  describe the contents of a PDF that was not read.
- When a claim looks wrong, flag it with the reason. Correcting it requires
  checking the source; without that, keep the original wording and mark it.
- Missing evidence gets a visible placeholder (`[sitasi diperlukan]`,
  `[citation needed]`), never an invented reference.

## Claim strength

Keep each claim at the strength its design supports:

| Evidence | Suitable verbs (EN) | Padanan (ID) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation, description | observed, reported, found | teramati, dilaporkan, ditemukan |
| Correlation, association | is associated with, correlates with | berhubungan dengan, berkorelasi dengan |
| Prediction or model output | predicts, estimates | memprediksi, memperkirakan |
| Inference from results | suggests, indicates | menunjukkan, mengindikasikan |
| Hypothesis | is expected to, is hypothesized to | diduga, dihipotesiskan |
| Causal design (for example randomized experiment) | causes, increases, reduces | menyebabkan, meningkatkan, menurunkan |

Moving a claim down this table is allowed only to match the source. Moving it up
needs evidence the source actually provides.

## Sources and recency

When the task includes choosing or describing sources, the research owner's
rules decide. As general guidance:

- Prefer peer-reviewed, relevant and methodologically sound work; venue
  reputation alone does not guarantee quality, and a credible preprint or
  repository copy is not excluded only for where it is hosted.
- Distinguish preprint, published version, revised edition and open-access copy,
  and say which one was used.
- Read "recent" windows against the actual task date. Keep foundational work for
  established methods; do not swap a classic citation for a newer one without a
  reason.
- Verify bibliographic details against the source or a reliable record. Never
  invent authors, titles, years, DOIs, page numbers, URLs or quotations.

## Style guides and institutions

Follow the requested citation style and the campus template. APA guidance is a
useful reference for APA-based work, not a replacement for institutional rules.
Where the institution sets italics, numbering, heading levels, person (for
example "penulis" versus "peneliti") or tense, apply it consistently.

## Project-specific rules

Approved titles, research questions, variables, datasets, settings, prohibited
terms and supervisor decisions belong to the user's research project, not to
this skill. Read them from the project when provided; do not copy them into
reusable guidance or apply one student's decisions to another project.

## Register

- Indonesian academic prose: baku, impersonal by default, passive voice is common
  in methods, foreign terms italicized unless the institution says otherwise.
- English academic prose: formal, contractions avoided unless the style guide
  allows them, hedging matched to evidence, one spelling variety.
- Avoid inflated framing ("sangat krusial", "groundbreaking") that the evidence
  does not carry; state the specific contribution instead.

## Check before delivering

- Every citation in the original is still present and attached to the same claim.
- Numbers, statistics, tables and quotations match the source exactly.
- Limitations and uncertainty survive the edit.
- No new source, finding or approval appears without support.
- The required sections and depth are complete.
