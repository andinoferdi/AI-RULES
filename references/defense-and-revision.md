# Seminar, final defense and revision

Use for evidence-grounded oral preparation and examiner-feedback follow-up. Practice understanding and concise reasoning, not bluffing or defeating an examiner.

## Mode, protocol and brief

SEMINAR_PROPOSAL focuses on problem/gap/contribution, RQ/theory, planned design/sample/instrument/analysis, ethics/feasibility and scope; never pretend planned findings already exist. FINAL_DEFENSE adds actual execution, deviations from proposal, data/analysis, findings, discussion, conclusion, limitations and BAB I–V consistency. Refresh obsolete proposal state before final-defense preparation.

Use actual institution/program instructions for time, Q&A format, materials, rubric, public/closed format, language and committee instructions. Missing protocol stays UNKNOWN; offer generic preparation without inventing event rules. Academic-level adaptation follows institutional rubric, official expectations and supervisor guidance first. S1 application/coherence, S2 synthesis/independence and S3 original contribution are only ADAPTIVE_HEURISTIC tendencies, never universal requirements.

Build a compressed Defense Brief: title/one-sentence study, problem/importance/gap, contribution, RQ/objectives, theory/framework, actual or planned design/participants/data/instrument/analysis, final-defense findings/conclusions, limitations, strongest justification, weakest decision, boundaries, locked decisions and unresolved issues. Use it as the basis of Q&A.

## Dynamic questions and risk

Generate original thesis-specific questions across foundation, gap/contribution, literature/theory, RQ/objectives, design, sample/data/ethics, measurement, analysis, findings, discussion/conclusion, limitations/implications and consistency/process. Include clarification, method–finding linkage, literature and recruitment concerns. For sempro, findings questions concern planned evidence/readiness. Support 50+ questions when requested, without duplicating a proprietary bank or inventing thesis content to reach a count.

Attach thesis anchor, likely concern and priority; prioritize actual weak gap/novelty, vague constructs, unused theory, sampling/measurement weaknesses, method/analysis mismatch, missing ethics, unsupported causal/conclusion claims, null findings, deviations and unresolved feedback. Do not invent vulnerabilities or predict every examiner's question/motive. A hypothetical challenge should be labeled as practice.

## Interpret and answer

Possible intents: CLARIFY, JUSTIFY, EVIDENCE, CONSISTENCY, METHOD_FIT, ALTERNATIVE, BOUNDARY, CHALLENGE_ASSUMPTION, INTERPRETATION, CONTRIBUTION, LIMITATION, PROCESS, COUNTERFACTUAL, REVISION_SIGNAL. Multiple intents can coexist; these are tentative interpretations, not knowledge of personal motives. For compound questions, read fully, identify all subquestions and the primary concern, clarify material ambiguity and answer each without evading the difficult part.

Answer directly in one or two sentences, then relevant rationale, thesis evidence, boundary and optional close. A simple factual answer needs no five-part ritual. Justification connects research need, criteria, alternatives, chosen option, tradeoff and evidence/constraint; 'my supervisor said so' alone is no methodological explanation. Prefer actual thesis/proposal and data/analysis, verified literature, applicable guideline, documented decisions and bounded inference; do not invent source numbers, quotes, theories or authority.

Answer cards record question/intent, direct answer, rationale, evidence and page/section if known, boundary, alternative if challenged, likely follow-up, grounded confidence and unresolved evidence. They are reasoning scaffolds, not required scripts. Respectfully correct false premises using the actual thesis, then answer the clarified question. Acknowledge valid criticism and distinguish correction from disagreement.

For KNOWN answer normally; PARTIALLY_KNOWN answer the supported portion; UNKNOWN_BUT_DERIVABLE reason from the thesis while labeling inference; OUT_OF_SCOPE explain the boundary; UNKNOWN admit what would be needed. Never fabricate certainty. For blank/freeze, pause, paraphrase, identify intent, recall the relevant section and give one supported core point before expanding; avoid filler that conceals not knowing.

## Adaptive mock and readiness

Ask one question, wait for the user answer, assess it, then ask a targeted follow-up where needed. Do not immediately supply an ideal answer unless requested; give an improved blueprint after the thread. Follow-ups arise from the actual response's unsupported claim, vague term, missing evidence, tradeoff, inconsistency, limitation or overgeneralization. Vary wording and difficulty BASIC/STANDARD/CHALLENGING/ADVERSARIAL_ACADEMIC; the latter is critical scrutiny, not hostility.

Give descriptive feedback on directness, correctness, rationale, traceability, consistency, boundary awareness, clarity, concision and composure support. Numerical scores are optional when requested. Practice 30-second, 90-second, three-minute or protocol-length summaries as needed: problem → question → method → finding (or planned evidence for sempro) → bounded significance.

Check foundation explanation, defensible methods/alternatives, final findings including null/unexpected results and provenance, literature/limitations, cross-chapter consistency, practiced high-risk follow-ups without scripts and known protocol. Return DEFENSE_READY, READY_WITH_HIGH_RISK_ITEMS, NEEDS_REHEARSAL, NEEDS_THESIS_FIX or BLOCKED with observed evidence; no readiness claim from an untested question bank alone.

## Examiner feedback to revision

Record exact feedback/question and source separately from inferred issue, required revision, affected chapters/state, priority, ambiguity, confirmation needed and status. Do not rewrite a material decision from ambiguous criticism. Use the revision-impact route: sampling feedback affects frame/criteria/rationale, BAB III, limitations/generalization and conclusions; novelty feedback affects literature/gap/contribution and downstream claims. Refresh answer cards and final consistency after changes. Use [defense helpers](../assets/defense-templates.md) only as needed.
