these reviewed local snapshots. They are not original Andino inventions or upstream
endorsements. The complete source/asset/disposition audit lives on the repository's

Guide provenance names the source techniques it adapts. Supporting resources are
local; source repositories, upstream plugins, network access and startup hooks are
not required for runtime use. Optional external services remain conditional on real
availability and task authorization. Installation executes no guide examples.

Community policy expression from Superpowers CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md (CC BY-SA 4.0),
branding, its visual companion server, and anthropic-best-practices.md expression
with unresolved independent notice are excluded. General authoring/visual-comparison
ideas are independently expressed under Andino's existing ownership and permissions.

Updates are manual: compare the actual source bytes and notices, review every changed
technique/resource against its guide, update the source/ownership matrices on main,
run source reconciliation, procedural regressions and disposable installer checks,
then obtain publication authorization. Do not fetch or auto-sync source at runtime.
