# Advanced methodology and integrity

Use only for decisions needing deeper coherence, data-source/framework reasoning, ethics/governance or publication due diligence. Do not activate every layer for a simple thesis task.

## Decision stack and coherence

An original adaptable stack considers consequential philosophy/assumptions; deductive, inductive, abductive or combined theory development; methodological choice; strategy/design; time horizon; and techniques/procedures including quality/ethics. A Research Onion diagram or fixed layer count is not a universal requirement. Each included layer must change an actual decision. Do not retrofit a philosophical label merely to justify a preselected test.

Check RQ → assumptions where material → theory approach → methodology → design/time → data/selection → collection/measurement → analysis → inference. A one-shot cross-sectional design cannot by itself describe within-person change over time. Preserve locked choices but surface contradictions and alternatives. Return COHERENT, COHERENT_WITH_RISK, NEEDS_ALIGNMENT or BLOCKED with evidence and affected dependency.

For gap spotting vs problematization, use the problem/RQ reference's distinction. Identify literature, the specific challenged assumption, its prevalence/materiality, contrary evidence, alternative lens, feasible question and risks. More advanced terminology is not a better contribution. Use reading-card comparisons for synthesis/SOTA: established knowledge, theories, methods/data, consistent/disputed findings, shifts, unresolved/weak areas and the study's position. Record INITIAL, DEVELOPING, EVIDENCE_SUFFICIENT_FOR_PROPOSAL or NEEDS_MORE_LITERATURE as SOTA maturity, not field status.

## Data-source decision

First ask whether existing data can answer the RQ with adequate construct, population/context/time fit and authorized ethical use. If not, identify the precise missing evidence and a feasible primary technique. Combining sources needs an explicit contribution to answerability and a compatible linkage plan. Return PRIMARY_ONLY, SECONDARY_ONLY, PRIMARY_PLUS_SECONDARY or NEEDS_REDESIGN and explain alternatives/limitations.

Primary technique families are options, not a prescribed count: questionnaire; structured interview; in-depth/semi-structured interview; focus group; non-participant observation; participant fieldwork; experiment/quasi-experiment; test/direct measurement; diary/experience sampling; task/think-aloud/workshop/co-design. Match breadth, lived meaning, group interaction, actual behavior, intervention effect, repeated process or usability to the evidence required, including access, burden and ethics.

Secondary candidates include statistics/census, public administration, organizational records, survey/panel archives, research repositories, operational/platform logs, market/financial data, legal/policy documents, media/web archives and institutional/document collections. Inspect original purpose, provenance, population/coverage, definitions, missingness/quality, time fit, license/access, consent/privacy and linkage/reidentification risk. Easy access alone does not prove fitness; public availability does not erase ethics.

## Theory, framework and analysis

Trace source-grounded theory → concepts/constructs → mechanism/explanatory relation → boundary/context → RQ → optional proposition/hypothesis → operationalization/data → analysis. Explain every framework arrow. Mediation, moderation, difference, association or time relationships require theory/design support, not cosmetic complexity. Hypotheses number 0..N, never a compulsory four; exploratory qualitative studies remain valid without statistical hypotheses.

For each applicable hypothesis retain ID, statement, theoretical/empirical basis, constructs, justified direction, mechanism, boundary, measurement and analysis/test. Keep prior versus exploratory status explicit. Quantitative analysis selection and diagnostics are owned by quantitative planning; do not duplicate a universal testing sequence here. Content/construct/criterion validity, inter-rater reliability, invariance and adaptation checks depend on measurement purpose and model, not a ritual alpha calculation.

## Ethics and governance gate

Apply the shared evidence/integrity rules to the actual design. Examine capacity/vulnerability, recruitment/incentives/coercion, power imbalance, withdrawal and applicable consent; physical, psychological, social, reputational, economic and informational risks; minimization, confidentiality vs anonymity/pseudonymity, storage/access, retention/transfer/deletion; recording, deception/debriefing, intervention and incidental-findings issues where relevant. Identify institutional review, permissions, policy and data-use terms from authoritative current sources. Never declare review unnecessary or obtained without evidence. Do not provide jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions from generic guidance.

For secondary/web data inspect original consent/terms, authorized access, reasonable privacy expectations, sensitive attributes, linkage/reidentification and publication risk. Keep unresolved material obligations visible before execution. Record authorship, conflicts of interest, accurate citation, transparent methods and auditability; no selective reporting or misleading data/image manipulation. Planning remains possible while affected collection/use awaits a real approval.

## Journal/publisher due diligence

When requested, verify current venue scope, publisher/contact/domain identity, editorial board, peer review, fee/APC and license transparency, preservation, identifiers, indexing claims at the actual index, ethics/correction policies, solicitation and turnaround claims, and institutional guidance. Record each source/date and distinguish VERIFIED_POSITIVE_SIGNALS, RED_FLAGS, UNVERIFIED_CLAIMS and DUE_DILIGENCE_INCOMPLETE. Use independent checks such as Think. Check. Submit. when available. One invitation, blacklist entry or indexing logo is insufficient for a definitive predatory label. Missing access yields an incomplete assessment, not a confident accusation or endorsement.

## Advanced readiness

Check coherent design/time/data/analysis, verified literature comparison and bounded SOTA, explicit gap strategy, meaningful theory/mechanisms, appropriate hypotheses, ethics/access/privacy and venue checks if requested. Return ADVANCED_READY, ADVANCED_READY_WITH_OPEN_ITEMS, NEEDS_METHOD_ALIGNMENT, NEEDS_EVIDENCE or BLOCKED, naming what changes the decision. Use [methodology helpers](../assets/methodology-integrity-templates.md) proportionately.
