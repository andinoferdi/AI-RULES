# Results, discussion and conclusion

Use for post-data writing and BAB IV–V review. Research evidence must precede empirical prose; a proposal or proposed analysis is not a completed result.

## Results readiness and Finding Registry

Inspect actual data/source, known analysis method, executed/reproducibly executable analysis, inspectable outputs and RQ/objective mapping. Execute authorized feasible analysis before claiming its output. READY means the requested findings are supported; PARTIALLY_READY permits only supported parts; NOT_READY permits structure/evidence/analysis planning, never invented numbers, participant quotes, themes or findings.

For each material finding register ID, RQ/objective, source data/artifact, analysis method and exact output reference, finding statement, direction/pattern, estimate/theme, uncertainty, statistical evidence where relevant, substantive importance, table/figure, and interpretation status. Finding states are VERIFIED_FINDING, PROVISIONAL_FINDING, NEEDS_ANALYSIS, NEEDS_VERIFICATION or NOT_APPLICABLE; they are not ResearchState field statuses. VERIFIED_FINDING requires provenance and inspected evidence. The registry is the writing source of truth, with artifact version/locator retained.

## Present the actual findings

Follow guideline-defined separate/combined structure while preserving observation → interpretation → literature comparison → implication as distinct functions. Organize by RQ/objective or justified analysis sequence; lead with the main finding and use prose to explain patterns rather than reading every table cell aloud. Separate Results should not become another literature review or premature conclusion.

- Quantitative: use actual estimates, uncertainty, test statistics/p-values if used, units/sample base, relevant diagnostics and planned/material analyses. Apply the quantitative reference's interpretation and causal limits.
- Qualitative: trace themes/categories/processes to actual coding/source context and available quotations; preserve variation and material negative cases. Do not invent representative quotes or force percentages into a design that does not need them.
- Mixed: show each strand's evidence and convergence, complementarity or divergence. Explain actual integration and how sequential stages informed each other; two disconnected results sections are insufficient.
- R&D: separate requirements, artifact/development, expert/user validation, functional/usability/performance/effectiveness evidence and revisions. Product completion alone proves no effectiveness claim.

Tables/figures need guideline-fit captions, units, labels, base/sample and defined abbreviations, with source/output provenance and text references. Do not fabricate data or distort axes/scales. Reconcile all values, labels and sample bases with the underlying analysis.

## Discussion and claim gates

For each material finding explain meaning for the RQ, relation to theory, fair comparison with relevant supporting and contrasting literature, plausible reasons for agreement/disagreement, uncertainty/limitations and bounded implications. Discussion starts with findings, not generic literature prose or new undisclosed data.

Report material expected, null, unexpected and contradictory findings. Possible explanations may involve design, measurement, sample/context, confounding, randomness or implementation; label each explanation VERIFIED, PLAUSIBLE or UNKNOWN according to its evidence. These are interpretation labels. Do not manufacture a causal story to rescue a hypothesis or cherry-pick literature.

Before causal wording inspect design, intervention/comparison, temporal ordering, confounding/identification, analysis and limitations. Association/descriptive evidence stays at that scope. Statistical evidence, magnitude, uncertainty and practical importance are different; p below a threshold does not mean a large or important effect, and non-significance alone does not establish absence.

## Conclusion and final gate

Map every RQ/objective → finding IDs → discussed meaning → bounded conclusion answer. Each conclusion point must directly and explicitly address its corresponding RQ without omitting any question or answering beyond what the empirical evidence proves. Do not introduce a new result, statistic, theory or citation to change interpretation at the conclusion stage; locate it appropriately upstream and re-review. Special institutional structure never removes data lineage. Be concise and no broader than population/context/design permits.

Limitations must describe actual design/data/measurement/sample/analysis/context constraints and their effect on interpretation/generalization. Separate real limitations from mitigated risks and possible improvements. Derive theoretical, practical or methodological implications from specific findings; local results do not automatically justify broad policy.

Recommendations identify trigger evidence/limitation/unresolved question, target actor, proposed action and why it follows. Never supply polished generic recommendations (such as 'tambahkan variabel' or 'perbanyak sampel') when empirical findings or real limitations are absent, even with a trailing caveat; request the missing basis or return only an unfilled conditional scaffold (trigger finding/limitation → target actor → proposed action). Check actual outputs, provenance, material findings, visual/text consistency, literature fairness, claim boundaries, all RQ answers, no new results, supported implications/recommendations and BAB I–V alignment. Return READY_FOR_REVIEW, READY_WITH_OPEN_ITEMS or NOT_READY with unresolved items. Use [result traceability helpers](../assets/results-traceability-templates.md); propagate changed findings through the revision route.
