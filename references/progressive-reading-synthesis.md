# Progressive reading and synthesis

Use for reading a paper, comparing sources, theory/gap evidence and discussion comparators. This is an original four-stage workflow, not a reproduction of proprietary stages or a promise of five-minute comprehension.

## Reading depth follows evidence need

| Stage | Inspect | Return / move deeper when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Relevance scan | Title, abstract, keywords, metadata, high-level question/context. | Relevance, probable role, next depth. Ambiguity goes to more evidence. |
| 2 — Structure and claim map | Problem/RQ, headings, figures/tables, conclusions, accessible limitations and relevant references. | Main claims, structure, key concepts, candidate evidence. Do not treat candidate claims as verified findings. |
| 3 — Method and evidence read | Design, participants/sample, instruments/measurement, collection, analysis, actual results/uncertainty, limitations/bias. | Verify the particular claim to be used and its caveats; source access must support this depth. |
| 4 — Synthesis and research integration | What is established/unresolved, supporting/contrasting studies, theoretical/method role and fit to the user's RQ. | Reading card, comparison entry and traceable reusable note. |

Use a proportionate timebox and record the stage actually completed. An abstract cannot verify full-text instrument, analysis, quotation or causal details. Keep ABSTRACT_ONLY as access-depth metadata; use NEEDS_EVIDENCE for a ResearchState field needing more. If only an AI summary is available, use it to locate the original; do not create a citation-ready evidence card as if the original were read.

## Paper Reading Card and claim provenance

Capture verified citation/identifier/link, access and verification status, role/relevance, problem/RQ, theory, design, population/sample/context, measurement, analysis, main results, uncertainty, limitations, author interpretation, agent evidence note, support/contrast, gap signal, reusable note, locator and completed stage. Omit unavailable details or label them explicitly; never invent missing bibliographic fields.

Every material claim follows claim → source identity/version → exact supporting section/result → depth/evidence status → interpretation boundary. Keep reported data, author's explanation and agent inference separate. Direct quotations require exact original wording and locator where available, minimal length and preserved context; a paraphrase or AI summary cannot become a direct quote.

Check material corrections/retractions when tools allow. Record NO_KNOWN_ISSUE, CORRECTION_EXISTS, RETRACTED or STATUS_UNVERIFIED with check date/source; these describe source status, not field status. No-known-issue is bounded by the check, not an eternal guarantee. Reassess affected claims; do not silently use retracted findings as ordinary supporting evidence.

## Chaining and synthesis

Use backward references and forward citing papers for a named purpose: terminology, seminal work, follow-up/replication or contradiction. Log seed, direction, tool/date, discovered record and decision; apply normal eligibility to new records. Stop when the current evidence need is met or a justified time/access boundary is reached; document remaining uncertainty. Chaining supplements rather than substitutes for reproducible systematic searching.

Move from cards to a comparison matrix, then themes/patterns, agreements/disagreements, methodological differences, evidence quality/limitations and bounded gap/SOTA conclusions. Identify the source set supporting each synthesis claim. Explain whether disagreement may follow context, measurement, design or uncertainty; do not choose only papers matching a favored conclusion. An annotated bibliography is input, not the final synthesis.

Run literature readiness across search coverage/log, screening rationale, sufficient reading depth, extraction provenance, comparisons and outstanding claims. Return what is ready to use, what remains provisional and the precise next search/read. A gap claim is not ready when source coverage or comparison is inadequate. The [literature templates](../assets/literature-templates.md) keep these distinctions visible.
