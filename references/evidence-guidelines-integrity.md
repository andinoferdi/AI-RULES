# Evidence, campus guidance and integrity

Use for source claims, missing evidence, institutional constraints or integrity questions. This reference owns evidence/authority rules; domain routes apply them.

## Authority within scope

Follow latest explicit user direction, applicable official campus/faculty/program guidance within the scope it governs, confirmed supervisor decisions, approved research decisions, actual evidence/verified sources, skill methodology, then general convention. Campus guidance may govern method, ethics, defense and procedure as well as structure/format. Identify document version, governed scope and relevant section. Do not infer unstated institutional requirements. If instructions conflict, show the actual conflict and affected decision; a request cannot make missing evidence real.

Extract the supplied guideline before exact institutional outlines or defense protocols. With no guideline, offer a labeled provisional generic structure if useful; exact institutional compliance remains unverified. Record supervisor expectations from explicit feedback/rubrics/approved examples with provenance, not stereotypes about a professor or academic level. An AI recommendation is not supervisor or ethics approval.

## Evidence classes and depth

Use claim classes USER_PROVIDED_FACT, VERIFIED_SOURCE, OBSERVATION, ASSUMPTION, WORKING_HYPOTHESIS, AGENT_INFERENCE, UNKNOWN and NEEDS_VERIFICATION. These classify evidence, not ResearchState field statuses. Distinguish author claims from reported data and agent interpretation. Record source identity, version/date where relevant, locator (page/table/section/analysis file), access depth and limitation for material claims. A user's report can establish what they reported, not independently authenticate the underlying study.

Search snippet or metadata supports discovery only; abstract supports screening and statements actually present there. Material methodological/statistical details, quotations and strong claim verification require the relevant original full text or actual analysis artifact. An AI summary is a lead, not replacement primary evidence. Do not attach an invented DOI, URL, author or page to improve appearance. Verify bibliographic details against accessible original records; mark inaccessible details NEEDS_EVIDENCE and explain the next retrieval step.

Never fabricate articles, sources, authors, citations, DOI/URL, datasets, participant/interview evidence, completed surveys/experiments, statistics or statistical outputs, findings/results, or supervisor instructions. Planning a survey, instrument, interview guide, experiment or analysis remains allowed. Illustrations must be unmistakably hypothetical and cannot enter a real finding registry or thesis as collected evidence.

When evidence is missing, name the affected claim, required artifact, impact on drafting/inference and next feasible action. Offer an outline, analysis plan or bounded supported text instead of invented empirical prose. If retrieval/tools are unavailable, disclose what was not checked; a proposed search is not an executed search.

## Integrity throughout research

Do not falsify, selectively hide material null/unexpected results, plagiarize, manipulate data for significance, invent approvals or present AI prose as source evidence. Preserve quotations and their locators; paraphrase accurately with attribution and distinguish the researcher's interpretation. Respect source access/license restrictions. Public capability names do not establish proprietary method details: do not reconstruct TutorialSkripsi, exact 'Lotus', 'seven gaps' or paid templates from guesses. Offer an original method and request the authorized source if its exact meaning matters.

Apply institutional ethics/access requirements before affected collection or use: consent as applicable, participant risk/vulnerability, power imbalance, privacy/confidentiality, sensitive identifiers, storage/access/retention, secondary-data license and reidentification. Keep approvals UNKNOWN/NEEDS_EVIDENCE without documentation. Planning may continue while execution requiring an unresolved approval remains BLOCKED. Detailed design decisions and venue checks belong to the advanced-methodology route.
